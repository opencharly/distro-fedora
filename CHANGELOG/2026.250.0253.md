## RCA (why the two preempt beds are restructured, not just re-asserted)

The unroll mechanically promoted the sorted-first member of each former group to the bed root. For the two preempt beds that member is the PREEMPTIBLE HOLDER — the workload the bed's own claimant member legitimately stops mid-run. The pre-migration beds were deliberately ROOT-LESS ("no workload root, so the runner's pre-acquire is a no-op") for exactly this reason. Live proof, run 1:

- check-preempt-live-pod: bring-up-members stopped the promoted root ("preempt: stopping holder \"check-preempt-live-pod\" to free test-lock"); check-live then hard-failed at plugin-pod's root-liveness gate (`container charly-check-preempt-live-pod is not running`, candy/plugin-pod service_resolve.go resolveServiceInit).
- check-preempt-vm-live: first check-live passed on timing (slow VM boot), but the taker's re-bring-up acquire stopped the root VM and the run hung before check-live-rebuild (reproduced twice, killed after RCA).

Fix: a BENIGN check-pod bed root (no arbiter fields — keeps the runner's pre-acquire a no-op) + the holder restored as a MEMBER (`preempt-holder` / `preempt-vm-holder`) + the claimant member. sortedMemberKeys ordering (…h… < …t…) is unchanged, coverage is identical to the pre-migration group, and the assertions now prove the member-vs-member live preemption against the member identity.
